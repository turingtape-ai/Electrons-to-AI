A battery at one end, a wire, and at the other end something that moves when current flows: a needle that twitches, a lever that clicks. The single yes from Chapter 1 now travels inside copper rather than across a line of sight, and for the first time a message does not care whether it is night or foggy.

This chapter covers how that worked in practice, why wires and poles spread faster than towers ever did, and what it meant for a message to arrive before the weather.

The interactive will let you close a switch on one side of the screen and watch a sounder click on the other.
