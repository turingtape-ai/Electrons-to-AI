A single processor can only do one thing at a time, yet a computer appears to do many. This chapter introduces the program that manages all the others: slicing time, fencing off memory, naming files, and speaking to the hardware so that nobody else has to.

The interactive will show a scheduler handing out slices of time to several programs.
