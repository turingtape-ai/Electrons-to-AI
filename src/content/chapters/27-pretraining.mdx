No one labels the training text. The model simply covers up the next word and guesses, billions of times. This chapter explains that setup, what the model is scored on, and why guessing the next word well ends up requiring facts, grammar, and a kind of reasoning. It also asks what it does not require.

The interactive will show a model's probabilities for the next word as you type.
