Wiring transistors together by hand has a limit. This chapter introduces the idea of printing a whole circuit, switches and wires together, onto one surface using light and masks, so that a chip is in effect a photograph of a design. Making many at once is what made each one cheap.

The interactive will let you stack masks to build a single gate.
