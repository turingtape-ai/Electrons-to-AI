This chapter assembles the transformer from parts already built: attention from Chapter 24, small weighted-vote networks from Part VI, and a way of telling the model where in the sentence each word sits. It explains why many layers help and what each layer has to work with.

The interactive will trace one word's representation through a stack of layers.
