Sentences arrive in order, and order matters. This chapter introduces networks that read one word at a time and carry a summary forward, explains why that summary tends to forget the beginning of a long sentence, and sets up the problem that attention was invented to solve.

The interactive will show a memory vector changing as words arrive.
