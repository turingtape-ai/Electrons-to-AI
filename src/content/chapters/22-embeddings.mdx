A network works on numbers, and a word is not a number. This chapter explains how words are given coordinates, how those coordinates are learned rather than chosen, and what it means that the arrow from *Paris* to *France* resembles the arrow from *Rome* to *Italy*.

The interactive will be a map of words you can explore.
