Numbers written with only two digits, so that a row of lit-or-dark lamps can hold one. Then a small arrangement of gates that adds two single digits and remembers the carry, and a row of those arrangements that adds numbers of any size, with the carry rippling along like a signal down a chain.

The interactive will be an adder you can feed two numbers into, watching each carry happen.
