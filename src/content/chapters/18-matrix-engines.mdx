Multiplying rows of numbers by columns of numbers and adding up: that is what graphics is made of, and it is also what learning turns out to be made of. This chapter introduces the matrix as a grid of weighted sums, and explains why the graphics card was ready for Part VI before anyone had planned it.

The interactive will animate a matrix multiplication one cell at a time.
