Rather than squeezing a whole sentence into one summary, let each word ask a question of every other word and take a weighted average of the answers. This chapter builds attention as a lookup, with queries, keys and values told in plain terms, and explains why it can be computed for every word at once.

The interactive will show attention weights lighting up across a sentence you type.
