Numbers are a poor language for people. This chapter follows the move from raw instructions to names, from names to languages with arithmetic and structure, and explains step by step what a compiler does to turn a line a person wrote into a list of numbers the machine can run.

The interactive will show an expression being translated into instructions as you type it.
