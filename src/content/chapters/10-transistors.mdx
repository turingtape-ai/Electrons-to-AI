Some materials can be persuaded to conduct or not conduct depending on a small push from the side. This chapter explains that idea without equations, introduces the transistor as a switch made of such material, and explains why small and cold mattered more than anyone first guessed.

The interactive will treat a transistor as a valve you can open and close with a slider.
