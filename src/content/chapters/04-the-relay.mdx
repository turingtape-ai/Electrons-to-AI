A current that has travelled a long way through a thin wire grows too weak to click a sounder. But it can still pull a small iron lever, and that lever can close a fresh circuit with a fresh battery behind it. The beacon chain returns, in brass and iron, remaking the signal station by station.

Then the chapter turns the idea around. If a switch can control a switch, switches can be arranged to make decisions, and Part II begins.

The interactive will let you build a relay from a coil, a pivoting lever and a contact, then chain several together.
