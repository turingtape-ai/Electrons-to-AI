Put the previous two chapters in a loop: guess, measure the error, assign blame, nudge every weight a little, repeat with the next example. This chapter covers what the loop needs, how big the nudges should be, why it works at all, and how it can go wrong by learning the examples instead of the pattern.

The interactive will show a boundary moving while the error falls.
