Take several inputs, multiply each by how much you trust it, add them up, and say yes if the total passes a line. This chapter introduces that machine, the perceptron, shows what it can and cannot tell apart, and makes the word *weight* mean something concrete.

The interactive will let you move the weights and watch the dividing line swing.
