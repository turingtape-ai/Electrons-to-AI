A network of weighted votes gives a wrong answer. Which of the thousands of weights should change, and by how much? This chapter explains the idea of blame flowing backward through the layers, each weight receiving its share, without calculus and without hiding what the calculus is for.

The interactive will push a single error back through a small network and show each weight's share.
