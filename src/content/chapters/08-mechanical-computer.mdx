With arithmetic and memory in hand, the remaining piece is a mechanism that reads an instruction, does what it says, and moves to the next one. This chapter tells that story through Charles Babbage's never-finished engines and the relay machines of the 1930s and 40s, and introduces the loop at the heart of every computer since: fetch, decode, do, repeat.

The interactive will let you step through a short list of instructions and watch the machine's state change.
