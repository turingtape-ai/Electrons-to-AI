A model that continues text will continue anything, helpfully or not. This chapter covers the steps that turn it into something that answers: examples of good answers, a second model trained to guess which of two answers people prefer, and a training loop that nudges the first model toward what the second rewards. It is honest about what this changes and what it leaves alone.

The interactive will let you rank two answers and watch a preference model shift.
