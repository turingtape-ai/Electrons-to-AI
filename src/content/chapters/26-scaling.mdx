The transformer in Chapter 25 is small. This chapter is about making it large: more layers, more weights, more text, more graphics cards, and the surprisingly regular way its errors fall as it grows. It also looks at what people claimed appeared suddenly along the way, and at how to read those claims carefully.

The interactive will be a scaling slider connecting size, data and error.
